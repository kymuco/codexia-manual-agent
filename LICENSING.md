## Public license model

The PolyForm Perimeter public license permits use, modification, and distribution for permitted purposes. Its central product boundary is the license's noncompete rule: a permitted purpose does not include providing to others a product that competes with the software.

The exact definitions and conditions in [`LICENSE`](LICENSE) control. This document is only a project-policy summary and does not replace, narrow, or expand those terms.

Internal professional or business use is not prohibited merely because it is commercial. The public license also permits modification and distribution when the use remains within its permitted-purpose boundary and all notice requirements are satisfied.

Because the public license restricts competing use, Codexia is source-available rather than OSI open-source software.

## Contributions and relicensing

Maintaining the ability to offer the source-available distribution and separately negotiated commercial licenses requires clear copyright authority over accepted contributions.

Until a contributor-agreement process is published, unsolicited code or documentation contributions are not accepted for inclusion in the canonical Codexia distribution. Bug reports, design discussion, reproducible test cases, and responsible security reports remain welcome. A maintainer may explicitly invite a contribution under separate written contributor terms.

Do not interpret submission of an issue, discussion, test case, or pull request as granting Codexia an additional relicensing right beyond rights that are explicitly agreed in writing.
