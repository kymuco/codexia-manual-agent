# M6.4 Explicit Non-Claims

M6.4 proves an exact dynamic human-attention decision surface. It does not claim:

- background scheduling or unattended work supervision;
- notification delivery or companion integration;
- learned attention preferences or multi-user attention profiles;
- that reversibility, impact, or alternative shape are objective physical facts rather than Codexia cognition outputs;
- infallible semantic interpretation of human attention constraints;
- local process, filesystem, Git, network, provider-send, or merge authority;
- that `KEEP_MOVING` authorizes a rejected, revision-required, or otherwise unauthorized action;
- that M6.2 semantic admission is equivalent to execution permission;
- cryptographic proof of the physical human behind HUMAN-attributed work statements;
- automatic extraction of all attention signals from arbitrary live ChatGPT prose;
- durable multi-work recovery across supervisor restarts.

Those boundaries remain explicit so M6.4 cannot be mistaken for the later M6.5–M6.7 runtime.
